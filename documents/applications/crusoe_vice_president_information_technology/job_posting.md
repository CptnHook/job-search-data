About the Role:

You will own and lead Crusoe's IT organization, spanning IT Service Desk, IT Systems Engineering, and Enterprise Technology functions, with direct accountability for the multi-year enterprise systems strategy and technical roadmap. This is a leader-of-leaders role: you will manage a team that includes Director-level direct reports, and you will personally own large-scale,enterprise systems transformations, ERP, supply chain, construction, and manufacturing systems among them, that support Crusoe's vertically-integrated strategy. You will define technology strategy and target-state architecture, make critical build-vs-buy decisions, and drive AI and agentic automation adoption across IT operations at scale.



What You'll Be Working On:

 - Lead the Service Desk, Systems Engineering, and Enterprise Technology functions, managing direct reports at the Director level

 - Own large-scale, enterprise systems and ERP transformations, migrating and modernizing platforms across Finance, Supply Chain, Manufacturing, and CRM

 - Manage the enterprise systems portfolio, including ERP platforms, CRM, Supply Chain, and other business systems

 - Define and own the multi-year enterprise technology roadmap, assessing current architecture, forming a clear technical opinion on the target state, and guiding technical teams and business stakeholders toward alignment and execution

 - Own global IT operations and service delivery, including SLAs, escalation models, and operational metrics

 - Champion AI adoption across IT operations, leveraging AI to automate workflows, increase team capacity, and reduce manual effort at scale

 - Oversee identity and access management (directory services, SSO, MFA, role-based access controls) and the complete IT asset management lifecycle

 - Develop and manage annual IT budgets while owning vendor performance management, ensuring the organization maximizes value from its technology investments and vendor relationships

 - Direct IT buildouts for new locations, manage onboarding and offboarding processes, and establish governance for change management, access controls, and system reliability



What You'll Bring to the Team:

 - Direct experience leading a large-scale, brownfield ERP or enterprise systems transformation, migrating off or replacing an existing system rather than a greenfield build, ideally extending beyond core financials into a major module such as manufacturing or supply chain; SAP experience strongly preferred

 - Proven, personal ownership of both Enterprise Technology and Systems Engineering functions, not adjacent oversight or a peer relationship to one of them

 - Demonstrated experience managing Director-level direct reports, true leader-of-leaders scope, not first-time or first-line people management

 - Experience operating in a manufacturing, construction, or data center environment, or in a compliance-regulated environment such as SOX or SOC 2; direct experience supporting a company through IPO readiness is a strong plus

 - Deep expertise in identity and access management, device management, endpoint platforms, system integrations, and APIs

 - Strong operational and financial acumen, with experience managing IT budgets, vendor relationships, and build-vs-buy tradeoffs

 - Executive presence with a proven ability to operate at both strategic and execution levels, communicating technology strategy, risk, and investment tradeoffs credibly to senior leadership

 - Experience leveraging AI to drive efficiency and automation across IT operations

 - 15+ years of experience across IT operations and enterprise systems, with 8+ years leading multi-function IT or enterprise technology organizations at scale

 - Bachelor's degree in Information Systems, Computer Science, or equivalent experience, and the ability to pass a background check
