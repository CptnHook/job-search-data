About the Role

As a Director, Field Engineering, you will lead a multi-team Field Engineering organization and own strategy, talent, operating model, and execution for a significant business area. You will scale customer and business impact through other leaders while influencing broader go-to-market, partner, Product, Engineering, and company priorities.

This role typically oversees a leadership team of approximately 3–5 leaders or an equivalent multi-team scope. Depending on the assignment, you may lead a broad business unit, multiple segments or countries, a major portfolio, or another strategically significant area.

The Impact You Will Have

- Build and lead a strong leadership team that attracts, develops, and retains exceptional Field Engineering talent.

- Own an end-to-end strategy across talent, customer coverage, technical priorities, operating model, execution, and business impact.

- Translate customer, market, competitive, and technical trends into clear priorities and 6–12+ month plans.

- Scale impact beyond individual teams through leaders, cross-functional GTM initiatives, partner ecosystems, and repeatable operating mechanisms.

- Build trusted relationships with senior customer stakeholders, including VP-level and equivalent audiences, and guide complex programs, escalations, and transformation roadmaps.

- Establish operating norms and inspection cadences across the organization that create visibility, accountability, predictable execution, and fast course correction.

- Shape resourcing and organizational design to support growth, customer outcomes, technical quality, and long-term business value.

- Upgrade hiring rigor, talent practices, leadership development, performance management, and succession planning across the organization.

- Influence broader Sales, Professional Services, Marketing, Partners, Product, Engineering, Support, and executive priorities through a clear point of view and strong cross-functional relationships.

- Partner with GTM Strategy &amp; Operations and other functions to create scalable tools, programs, dashboards, and mechanisms; use AI to drive one-to-many organizational impact.

- Act as a recognized resource on data, AI, cloud, customer transformation, and evolving market trends.

- Embed Databricks Culture Principles into hiring, development, recognition, decision-making, and daily operations while maintaining a high bar for performance and customer impact.

What We Look For

- Extensive experience leading technical customer-facing organizations in enterprise software, cloud, data, AI, analytics, technical consulting, or a related environment.

- Proven success leading through managers and building a high-performing leadership team.

- Track record of recruiting exceptional talent, developing leaders, addressing complex performance situations, and evolving organizational structure for scale.

- Strong strategic and business judgment, with the ability to connect customer and market insight to organizational priorities and measurable business outcomes

- Experience designing and running operating models across multiple teams, portfolios, segments, geographies, or countries.

- Demonstrated ability to influence senior leaders across Sales, Professional Services, Marketing, Partners, Product, Engineering, Support, and executive stakeholders.

- Executive-level customer-advisory skills, including the ability to guide complex programs, critical escalations, technical strategy, and long-term transformation roadmaps.

- Strong technical credibility across data platforms, analytics, AI, cloud architecture, governance, security, networking, and enterprise adoption, with the judgment to pressure-test complex approaches.

- An AI-first, builder mindset demonstrated through experimentation, adoption, and scalable application of AI to customer outcomes and organizational effectiveness.

- Demonstrated alignment with Databricks Culture Principles and a track record of preserving and strengthening culture during growth and change.

Basic Qualifications

- Experience leading multi-team technical customer-facing organizations and managing through other leaders.

- Experience owning strategy and execution for a significant business area, portfolio, geography, segment, or comparable scope.

- Experience leading complex customer transformations and cross-functional initiatives.

- Experience in data platforms, analytics, AI, cloud, software, technical consulting, or a related technical domain.

- Strong written, verbal, presentation, strategic-planning, and executive-stakeholder skills.

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

Preferred Qualifications

- Experience with Databricks or comparable data and AI platforms.

- Experience leading across multiple business units, countries, customer segments, or Field Engineering practices.

- Experience shaping broader GTM strategy, partner ecosystems, product priorities, or company-wide programs.

- Experience building leadership pipelines, succession plans, communities of practice, and scalable talent systems.

- Experience using AI, automation, analytics, or other technology to transform organizational operating models.
