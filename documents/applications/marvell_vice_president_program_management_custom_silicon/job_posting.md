Your Team, Your Impact
The Vice President of Program Management sets the vision and strategy for how Custom Cloud Compute (CCS) BU plans, governs, and delivers its most strategic programs. This executive owns the enterprise program portfolio, leads a multi-tier program management organization, and is accountable for predictable execution against business commitments. As a trusted advisor to the executive team, the VP translates corporate strategy into executable roadmaps and ensures investments, resources, and customer commitments are aligned.
What You Can Expect
- Define and execute the program management strategy, operating model, and organizational structure, aligned to business and customer priorities.
- Lead and develop a global organization of directors, senior managers, and program managers; build succession and a strong leadership bench.
- Own the enterprise program portfolio: prioritization, investment tradeoffs, capacity planning, and resource allocation with executive leadership.
- Establish enterprise-wide governance, including stage-gate frameworks, executive review cadences, and escalation mechanisms.
- Partner with the Group-level leadership and functional leaders across Engineering, Operations, Product, Finance, Sales, and Supply Chain to align roadmaps and commitments.
- Be accountable for delivery of strategic programs, including schedule, scope, quality, cost, and financial outcomes.
- Anticipate enterprise-level risks and dependencies; make and drive decisive tradeoff decisions across organizations.
- Provide executive and board-level reporting on portfolio health, performance metrics, and business impact.
- Serve as a senior executive sponsor for key customers and strategic partners, building trust and managing critical commitments.
- Own program budgets and headcount plans in partnership with Finance; drive operational efficiency and scalability.
- Drive a culture of accountability, transparency, and continuous improvement, including tools, data, and automation across the PMO.

What We're Looking For
- Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Business, or a related field; MBA or advanced degree strongly preferred.
- 20+ years of program management or operations experience, including 8+ years leading large teams and multiple management layers.
- Track record of delivering large-scale, complex, global programs with significant revenue or strategic impact.
- Experience establishing or transforming a PMO and enterprise governance at scale.
- Strong financial and business acumen, including P&L awareness, investment planning, and business-case development.
- Executive presence with proven ability to influence C-level leaders, customers, and partners.
- Demonstrated ability to lead through ambiguity, drive change, and build alignment across a matrixed organization.

Preferred Qualifications
- Experience in semiconductor, hardware, or technology product development and new product introduction (NPI).
- PMP, PgMP, or equivalent certification.
- Experience leading global, multi-site teams.
- Prior senior leadership of customer-facing strategic programs.

Success Measures
- Consistent on-time, on-budget delivery of strategic programs and roadmap commitments.
- Portfolio predictability and executive-level transparency.
- Customer and partner confidence in delivery.
- Leadership bench strength, engagement, and retention.
- Measurable improvement in operational efficiency and program maturity.

Competencies
- Strategic vision and enterprise thinking
- Executive leadership and organizational development
- Influence and cross-functional alignment
- Financial and data-driven decision making
- Decisiveness and accountability
- Clear, compelling executive communication
